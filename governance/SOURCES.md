# BlueLink Source and Provenance Register

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-029

## Research date and purpose

Public sources were checked on **20 October 2026** for the finalized brand publication. This register separates source observations from company-provided facts and standards written for this edition. It is an evidence record, not an open approval list.

The current website title was retrievable, but the full site body, stylesheets and legal pages were not available through the research tools. GitHub organization and release retrieval did not succeed. Those limitations do not establish that the repositories are private or absent. No complete website reconstruction, legal-title investigation or audit-to-bytecode comparison was performed.

## Company-provided evidence

Live deployment and audit-to-commit equivalence were not independently verified. No legal entity suffix, registration certificate, monitoring commitment or signed rights transfer is inferred.

**C02 - Original artwork.** Two 1024 x 1024 JPGs and two byte-identical 358 x 358 transparent PNG uploads. This edition preserves three unique originals and records both PNG upload names against their common hash. It does not claim a native vector or separate BLT artwork was supplied.

**C03 - Existing derivative exports.** Eight raster exports from the previously prepared package are preserved as derivatives. Their hashes and upscaling status are recorded. They are not recategorized as native masters.

## Measurements and edition standards

**M01 - File and color measurements.** The manifest, source map and color sample data record dimensions, bytes, alpha presence, hashes, sample coordinates and computed contrast ratios. Those properties were checked against the actual files.

**D01 - v1.0.0 documentation system.** Clear space, size limits, palette roles, font stacks, type hierarchy and operating procedures are standards defined by this finalized edition. They are not described as extracted historical website settings. Original logo geometry and pixels are preserved.
