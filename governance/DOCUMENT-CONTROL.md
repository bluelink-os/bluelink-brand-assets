# BlueLink Document Control Standard

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-016

## Purpose and scope

Keep artwork, public wording, permissions and release files traceable. This standard covers the brand repository, its handbook, machine-readable records and future updates. It defines responsibilities without inventing departments or naming an approver who did not make a recorded decision.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Brand authority | Decides identity, naming, visual standards and exceptions |
| Rights reviewer | Evaluates ownership evidence, permissions and third-party terms |
| Product representative | Confirms product names and the accuracy of technical references |
| Repository maintainer | Validates files, metadata, links, checksums and source changes |
| Publisher | Publishes the intended commit and records the actual release event |

One authorized person may hold more than one role. Record actual authority for each decision rather than assuming that every contributor can license a mark.

## Document states

For subsequent changes use `working` -> `under review` -> `final` -> `superseded` or `withdrawn`. Distribution is recorded separately as `packaged` or `published`. **The files delivered in this v1.0.0 package are final; creating the package does not create a GitHub publication event.**

A reusable blank form is an operational template, not an unfinished policy or proof of a completed approval.

## Control fields and precedence

The document register records ID, title, path, version, issue date and document state. The tagged source files are the controlled reference; the handbook is the illustrated companion. If a rendered summary and a detailed policy differ, use the detailed policy and issue a correction.

Evidence in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) distinguishes public-source observations, company-provided facts, direct measurements and standards defined by this edition. Preserve those distinctions when rewriting public copy.

## Change process

Use a branch or pull request with a clear reason, affected files and evidence. Check visual, legal and product impact. Update the changelog and metadata, regenerate integrity records and have the appropriate authority decide sensitive changes. Keep actual decision evidence in restricted storage and publish only nonsensitive references.

## Versioning and retention

Use a new major version for a materially changed identity or incompatible permission framework, a minor version for additional compatible artwork or guidance, and a patch for corrections that do not change identity or permissions. Do not replace already published assets under the same release identity.

Retain released files, decision references and change history. Identify superseded materials without suggesting that previously downloaded copies cease to exist. Keep confidential corporate records outside the public repository.

## Maintenance cadence

Check this library at each release, at least annually, and whenever a domain, contact, ownership, permission or product reference changes. This is an operating requirement, not a claim that automated monitoring has been enabled.
