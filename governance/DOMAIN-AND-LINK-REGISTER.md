## Maintenance rules

Use HTTPS references. Do not add wallet addresses or payment links to this brand register. After a domain or contact change, update company-controlled materials, request corrections from third parties and retain a dated record of the transition.

A third-party link correction cannot be completed by editing this repository. Recheck the SolidProof destination when recording a correction rather than asserting that it has already been changed.

A failed retrieval does not establish that a GitHub repository is private, missing or unsafe. The source register records the observation without guessing its cause.
