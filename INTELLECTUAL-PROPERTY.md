# BlueLink Intellectual Property Framework

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-004

## Purpose

Separate public brand identification from the private legal records that establish rights. This library identifies distributed assets and their permitted handling; it is not the place to publish confidential assignments or to manufacture ownership evidence.

## Rights categories

| Category | Treatment in this library |
|---|---|
| Brand names and product names | Controlled through naming and trademark-use rules; no registration claim is made by this edition. |
| Logo artwork and documentation | Distributed subject to applicable copyright, licenses and the repository rights notice. |
| Source code and smart contracts | Governed by the relevant product repository and its license; not relicensed here. |
| Domains and accounts | Maintained as communication and distribution references; not evidence of IP title by themselves. |
| Third-party material | Remains subject to its own rights; reference links do not confer a license. |

## Corporate records

Maintain original creator agreements, assignments, relevant employment or contractor terms, licenses and registration records in restricted corporate storage. Each public asset decision should point to a nonsensitive internal reference when such evidence is required. Keep personal addresses, signatures, identity documents and financial details out of the public repository.

A record of who uploaded a file is useful provenance but is not, on its own, proof that all necessary rights were transferred. Copyright in a work is also distinct from possession of a copy. Applicable law and the underlying instruments determine ownership and transfers. [S07]

## Domain transition

Use **bluelinkos.com** as the current website reference in new brand materials. Retain historical domain information where necessary to explain older documents. Updating a domain in a document or repository does not itself transfer copyright or establish trademark rights. [S06-S07]

## Public statements

Use [IP-OWNERSHIP-STATEMENT.md](documents/IP-OWNERSHIP-STATEMENT.md) for the limited public notice. Use the exact verified entity and registration details only when publishing a separate, supported legal record. Do not infer an LLC or corporation suffix, registration territory, creator identity or first-use date from these assets.

## Disputes and corrections

Send a rights concern to **ai@bluelinkos.com** with the asset path, nature of the concern and appropriate evidence. Keep sensitive evidence private. Preserve the relevant version, review the concern with the responsible representative and record any restriction, correction or removal through change control.
