# BlueLink Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-005

## Identity principles

Present BlueLink with visual consistency, readable information and accurate claims. The identity centers on the supplied interlocking blue-and-navy monogram. Its layered gradients and internal negative space are part of the artwork, not optional styling.

These are the **v1.0.0 standards for this brand library and new documentation**. Preserve the supplied logo pixels. The palette and layout rules standardize new materials; they are not a claim about previously deployed website styles.

## Naming and voice

Use **BlueLink** in general communications and **BlueLink Blockchain Services** when identifying the organization. Introduce **BlueLink Token (BLT)** in full, then use BLT where the meaning is clear. Keep descriptions precise and evidence-based. Avoid unsupported superlatives, guaranteed outcomes, implied licensing and descriptions of future capabilities as existing services.

For public company copy, use [COMPANY-OVERVIEW.md](../documents/COMPANY-OVERVIEW.md).

## Logo selection and layout

Choose the supplied light-background presentation for light placements and the dark-background presentation for dark placements. Use the transparent PNG only when the actual background preserves the complete mark's readability.

Keep at least **H/8** clear space on each side, where H is the visible monogram height. Use a visible height of at least **64 CSS px** for normal identity placements. Keep the source canvas intact and add layout margin where necessary. Do not crop, stretch, rotate, redraw or recolor the symbol.

Read [Logo Specifications](LOGO-SPECIFICATIONS.md) for pixel dimensions, small-size exceptions and print limits.

## Color

Use **Monogram Navy #11203F** for dark text, **Core Blue #155BBC** for primary accents, and **White #FFFFFF** for document backgrounds. Use **Sky Blue #3D90F0** and **Bright Highlight #4497F2** selectively, especially on **Dark Surface #11131F**.

These colors are sampled from the supplied raster artwork, with white added as a documentation neutral. They must not replace the original gradient with a flat fill. The light JPG's sampled background is #FEFEFE, not pure white. See [Color Palette](COLOR-PALETTE.md).

## Typography and accessibility

Use the system sans-serif and monospace stacks in [Typography](TYPOGRAPHY.md). Establish hierarchy with size, weight and whitespace rather than excessive colors or all-capital paragraphs. Keep body text readable and links distinguishable without relying on color alone.

Use normal text/background contrast of at least **4.5:1**, or **3:1** for large text under WCAG's definition. Check meaningful interface graphics separately. Logo-specific exceptions do not remove the need to make surrounding content accessible. [S08-S09]

## Product and partner relationships

Do not rename the corporate logo as an official BLT token icon. No separate product artwork is included in v1.0.0. Use product names in ordinary text and register future visual identities through the product branding process.

Partner lockups and third-party marks require the applicable permissions. Keep identities separate and do not imply endorsement or audit coverage through visual proximity.

## Publishing and maintenance

Use descriptive filenames and accessible alternative text. Select files from the manifest, not screenshots or scraped copies. Record changes, preserve originals and verify checksums before distribution. Rights-sensitive changes follow [Asset Approval](../governance/ASSET-APPROVAL-POLICY.md).
