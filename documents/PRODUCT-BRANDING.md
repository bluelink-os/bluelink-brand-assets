# BlueLink Product Branding Standard

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-013

## Brand architecture

**BlueLink** is the corporate brand represented by the supplied monogram. **BlueLink Token (BLT)** is the product identity referenced in this release. The brand repository is a shared identity resource, not the technical source of truth for every product.

Do not infer a separately released operating-system product from the domain name bluelinkos.com. Do not add exchanges, banking services, blockchains or other products to the released identity registry solely because they appear in historical promotional material.

## Naming

Use the full product name at first mention and its short form only when unambiguous. Retain the corporate spelling and capitalization. Distinguish a company statement, a token identifier and a software repository name; they serve different purposes.

Plain-text product naming is permitted as documentation. It is not an official graphical lockup. Do not place a newly typeset ticker inside the monogram or duplicate the corporate asset under a token filename as though it had been separately supplied.

## Artwork registration

A new product mark must have its source artwork, intended product, applicable rights evidence, native format, visual use rules and decision record. Register the asset path and SHA-256 in the manifest and add its relationship to the corporate identity in [ECOSYSTEM-ASSET-REGISTRY.md](ECOSYSTEM-ASSET-REGISTRY.md).

Use [PRODUCT-REGISTRATION.md](../templates/PRODUCT-REGISTRATION.md) to collect the necessary information. A roadmap item may have a text reference without receiving a live-product or logo designation.

## Lifecycle and technical separation

Record naming, artwork availability and technical lifecycle separately. A final logo does not prove product availability, and a deployed contract does not mean every repository file was audited. Technical releases, networks, verified contract addresses and audit-to-source comparisons belong in the product's own controlled records.

## v1.0.0 scope

The release distributes corporate artwork only. BLT has a text identity and source references, but no separate token artwork. Other ecosystem product logos are outside this release, not represented by empty claims of completeness.
