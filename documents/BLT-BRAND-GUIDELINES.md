# BlueLink Token (BLT) Brand Guidance

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-014

## Name and identity

Write **BlueLink Token (BLT)** at first mention and **BLT** thereafter when context is clear. Use ordinary text until a separate graphical identity is supplied and registered. The corporate monogram in this library must not be represented as a separately verified BLT icon.

## Audit wording

Use: **A BlueLink smart-contract security assessment is available from SolidProof. Consult the assessment for the specific files, hashes, findings and exclusions.**

Do not say that the brand repository, all ecosystem products, every related contract or a later software commit is audited on the basis of that link. SolidProof's published page limits scope and disclaims endorsement and guarantees. This library does not redistribute its badge or report. [S02]

## Deployment and transaction safety

Do not add an address from a partial audit label, a logo file, an unverified social post or a screenshot. Contract addresses, networks and transaction instructions belong in verified product documentation. A token logo, ticker or brand file is not an authentication mechanism.

No price, return, liquidity, regulatory-approval or blanket safety claim forms part of this brand guidance. The company-supplied production-release context is retained in provenance without being treated as independently verified deployment evidence.

## Artwork submission

Register any future BLT icon with native source, rights reference, intended network/listing use and asset hash. Keep its identity version separate from smart-contract versioning and from the corporate brand release.
