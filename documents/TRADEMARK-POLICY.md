# BlueLink Trademark Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-010

## Covered identifiers

This policy addresses use of **BlueLink**, **BlueLink Blockchain Services**, the corporate monogram and the product name **BlueLink Token (BLT)** in connection with this brand library. Listing a name here does not assert that it is registered in any territory or that every use of the same word is prohibited.

## Correct use

Use the supplied spelling and capitalization. Identify the actual organization or product without implying a broader relationship. Keep a third party's identity visibly distinct from BlueLink's identity. Do not register or present a domain, social account, application or product in a way that falsely suggests it is an official BlueLink channel.

Do not place a registered-trademark symbol next to a BlueLink identifier unless the relevant registration and use are verified. This edition makes no registration claim and does not add trademark symbols to the artwork. [S05]

## Permissions

A corporate mark is not a partner badge, endorsement, audit seal or certification. Written permission is required for uses that are not independently allowed by applicable law, the platform terms or an existing agreement. Describe the use precisely through [BRAND-AUTHORIZATION.md](BRAND-AUTHORIZATION.md).

The policy does not prohibit lawful commentary, criticism, reporting, comparative reference or other protected uses. Whether an exception applies depends on the circumstances and relevant law; do not turn a visual guideline into an unsupported infringement claim.

## Changes and enforcement

Keep records of written brand permissions and the scope of any registration relied on in a dispute. Escalate material misuse to the responsible company representative rather than making public accusations. Requests and concerns may be sent to **ai@bluelinkos.com**.

If a registration or rights change is later published, state its actual owner, territory, scope and record reference accurately. A domain change alone is not a trademark transfer. [S06]
