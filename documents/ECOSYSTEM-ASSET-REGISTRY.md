**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-015

## Registered identities in this release

| Identity ID | Public name | Relationship | Visual asset coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BL-CORPORATE` | BlueLink / BlueLink Blockchain Services | Corporate identity | Three unique supplied corporate originals plus eight documented derivatives |
| `BL-BLT` | BlueLink Token (BLT) | Product identity reference | Text naming and product references only; no standalone BLT logo in v1.0.0 |

The detailed corporate files are listed in [ASSET-MANIFEST.md](../ASSET-MANIFEST.md). The registry identifies artwork coverage; it is not a product launch register, token list, incorporation record or audit certificate.

## Field rules

Maintain an identity ID, public name, relationship to the corporate brand, corresponding asset IDs, current identity version and applicable source reference. Keep any technical lifecycle claim separate and supported by its product evidence. Never use artwork availability as a proxy for a product being live.

## Adding an identity

Record the proposed name, purpose, accountable representative, native artwork, rights evidence and public technical reference in [PRODUCT-REGISTRATION.md](../templates/PRODUCT-REGISTRATION.md). The asset approval process determines whether the new identity enters a release. Preserve previous identifiers when a product is renamed.

## Excluded claims

No additional product logo set or separately launched BlueLink OS product is represented by v1.0.0. Historical ecosystem descriptions and the current domain name do not establish a present product catalog. Add only documented identities; do not fill this registry with inferred product names.
