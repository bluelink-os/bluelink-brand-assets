## Public company profile

**BlueLink Blockchain Services** is the organization behind BlueLink, a blockchain and digital-asset brand focused on **tokenized real-world assets and digital markets**.

The BlueLink brand library provides corporate artwork, identity standards and public reference materials for consistent use across company communications and related projects. It separates corporate branding from product specifications, smart-contract code and confidential corporate records.

**BlueLink Token (BLT)** is the token identity referenced in the company's supplied product materials. Its technical repository and the separate SolidProof assessment are listed below. Readers should use the relevant product documentation for functionality, network details and any deployment information rather than infer those facts from branding.
