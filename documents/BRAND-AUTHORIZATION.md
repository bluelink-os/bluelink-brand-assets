# BlueLink Brand Authorization Procedure

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-012

## Request

Send a completed [Brand Use Request](../templates/BRAND-USE-REQUEST.md) to **ai@bluelinkos.com**. Identify the exact asset and version, requesting organization, planned use, audience, media, territory, dates and proposed modifications. Include a final placement preview where available.

The request itself is not permission. Do not publish a commercial partner treatment solely because the files are downloadable.

## Decision authority

Authorization must come from a representative with actual authority over the requested use. Routine formatting review by a repository maintainer is not authority to grant a trademark license. If a third-party mark is involved, obtain permission from that rights holder as well.

## Required authorization record

A written authorization must identify the parties, asset path or ID, applicable version, permitted use and media, dates or termination conditions, territory where relevant, allowed modifications and any attribution or quality requirements. It must record the representative's actual decision and authority; no completed approval is prefilled in this release.

The form in [ASSET-APPROVAL-RECORD.md](../templates/ASSET-APPROVAL-RECORD.md) supports internal asset decisions. A commercial licensing agreement may require separate terms suitable for the transaction.

## Publication control

Compare the final execution with the authorized preview. Confirm that the correct asset and any required disclaimer are used. Material changes in context, product, scale, territory or medium require a new decision where not already covered by the written permission.

Keep complete permissions in restricted storage. Public release notes should include only a nonsensitive decision reference where necessary. Do not upload signatures, private addresses or confidential commercial terms.

## Expiry, withdrawal and complaints

Follow the actual authorization terms and applicable law. Record restrictions or withdrawal requests without pretending that public copies can be erased. A change to a repository policy does not automatically extinguish rights already granted under an existing agreement.

No response-time guarantee, automatic approval rule or sublicensing permission is created by this procedure.
