# BlueLink Brand Usage Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-002

## Purpose

Maintain clear, accurate and consistent use of the BlueLink name, corporate monogram and product identity references. This policy applies to the assets distributed in this library and accompanies the rights notice in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Permission and presentation

| Use | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| BlueLink-managed website, repository or publication | Use the released asset and the internal publication process. |
| Partner marketing, commercial promotion, merchandise or a co-brand lockup | Obtain written authorization for the specific mark, placement, medium and duration, unless the use is independently permitted by law or an existing agreement. |
| Press, education, commentary or factual identification | Identify BlueLink accurately, avoid implying endorsement and respect applicable rights and legal exceptions. |
| Modified artwork, sublicensing or a new product identity | Obtain explicit written authorization; ordinary access to the files is not that authorization. |
| Personal or third-party channel | Do not present it as an official BlueLink account or company service. |

A download, GitHub fork, token purchase, unsolicited submission or absence of a response is not additional trademark permission.

## Visual rules

Preserve the monogram's proportions, orientation, internal spacing and gradients. Use the original JPG presentations as supplied; do not treat the light and dark images as interchangeable color conversions. Test transparent artwork against its actual background.

Maintain clear space of at least **one eighth of the visible mark height** on every side. For ordinary identity placement, keep the visible mark at least **64 CSS pixels high**. Compact icon use is addressed separately in [Logo Specifications](brand-guidelines/LOGO-SPECIFICATIONS.md).

Do not stretch, crop, rotate, recolor, redraw, outline or decorate the mark without authorization. Do not merge it into another logo or place it in a way that implies an unapproved commercial relationship.

## Claims and public identity

Write the corporate brand as **BlueLink**, the organization as **BlueLink Blockchain Services**, and the product at first mention as **BlueLink Token (BLT)**. Do not add legal entity suffixes, registered-trademark symbols, licensing claims or financial guarantees without the applicable records.

A brand image is not proof of a legitimate wallet, token contract, listing or transaction. A link to an audit is not a guarantee or a statement that every BlueLink product has been audited. Third-party logos and audit badges require their own permission and are not included in this library.

## Permission requests

Send requests to **ai@bluelinkos.com**, using [the request form](templates/BRAND-USE-REQUEST.md). Include the exact asset path, proposed use, audience, channels, dates and a preview. Written authorization must describe the permitted use and any limitations; [BRAND-AUTHORIZATION.md](documents/BRAND-AUTHORIZATION.md) defines the record.

Retain executed permissions in restricted corporate storage. Publish only nonsensitive references when documenting an asset decision. Report suspicious brand use privately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Precedence

Applicable law and existing written agreements control where they differ from this general policy. This document does not remove GitHub platform permissions or statutory exceptions. Sources: [S03-S04](governance/SOURCES.md).
