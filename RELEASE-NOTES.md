# BlueLink Official Brand Assets - v1.0.0

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-027

## Initial brand identity release

BlueLink Blockchain Services presents the v1.0.0 brand asset library: a centralized collection of corporate artwork, identity standards and public brand-use documentation.

## Included in this version

Three unique original corporate raster assets: a transparent PNG and the supplied light- and dark-background JPG presentations. Eight derived PNG exports provide icon sizes and background composites, with original-versus-derived and upscaling information recorded in the manifest.

The release also includes the digital documentation palette and typography, logo rules, company overview, brand rights and authorization policies, product naming guidance, governance procedures, an illustrated handbook, operational templates and SHA-256 verification tools.

## Asset scope

This is a corporate raster-and-documentation release. It does not include an original SVG master, monochrome artwork, editable wordmark or separately designated BLT or other product logo. No corporate asset is relabeled as token-specific artwork.
