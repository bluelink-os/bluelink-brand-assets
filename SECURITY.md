# BlueLink Brand Integrity and Security Reporting

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Issue date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Final  
**Organization:** BlueLink Blockchain Services | **Document ID:** BL-020

## Scope

Use this channel for suspicious brand assets, repository impersonation, tampered downloads, exposed credentials or misuse of company identity. This policy is not a smart-contract bug bounty or a promise of compensation.

## Private reporting

Email **ai@bluelinkos.com** with the subject `BlueLink brand integrity report`. Include the affected URL or file path, package version, observed behavior, relevant hash and a concise reproduction or screenshot where appropriate. Provide a way to contact you.

Do not include seed phrases, private keys, passwords, unnecessary personal information or a usable exploit against a live service. Do not publish confidential evidence in a public GitHub issue. A routine typo or broken relative link can be reported publicly without sensitive details.

## Response handling

The responsible representative should preserve the report, assess urgency, confirm facts and document any action. This policy does not promise 24-hour monitoring, a response deadline, a bounty or legal immunity. Escalate financial or account emergencies through the relevant service provider's established channels as well.

## Authenticity checks

Start from the company website and the referenced GitHub organization. Compare downloaded asset bytes with the published checksums. A matching checksum is useful only when obtained from a trusted reference; it is not independent proof of the publisher's identity or legal ownership.

Do not approve a wallet interaction or token purchase because a site displays the BlueLink monogram. Brand files do not verify a blockchain contract or transaction destination. No wallet connection is required to use this asset library.

## Remediation

For a confirmed repository problem, restrict or correct affected distribution, rotate any exposed secret through its real provider, publish an appropriate correction and preserve nonsensitive historical records. Do not promise that public copies can be recalled or treat every allegation as proven misuse.
